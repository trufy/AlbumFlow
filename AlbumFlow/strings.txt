PLUGIN_ALBUMFLOW_TIDAL_STREAMFIX
	DE	TIDAL: Stream statt Transcoding (Squeezebox Boom/Classic)
	EN	TIDAL: downgrade stream instead of transcoding (Squeezebox Boom/Classic)

PLUGIN_ALBUMFLOW_TIDAL_STREAMFIX_DESC
	DE	Experimentell. Verhindert, dass TIDAL-Titel auf älteren Squeezeboxen nach ca. 50 Sekunden abbrechen: Player, die kein Hi-Res abspielen können, bekommen den Stream direkt in LOSSLESS statt über eine Transcoding-Pipeline. Greift in das TIDAL-Plugin ein.
	EN	Experimental. Stops TIDAL tracks cutting out after about 50 seconds on older Squeezeboxes: players that can't play Hi-Res get a LOSSLESS stream directly instead of a transcoding pipeline. Patches the TIDAL plugin.

PLUGIN_ALBUMFLOW_TIDAL_AI_DESC

	EN	Experimental. Shows an "AI" badge on tracks TIDAL flags as AI generated. Patches the TIDAL plugin to keep the flag in its cache.
